# Brand: tone of voice and key phrases

Status: DRAFT v1, 2026-11-13. Drawn from the voice of useasterisk.com (asterisk-raw.md; full copy in docs/research/sources/useasterisk-site-copy.md).

## Voice
- **A thoughtful colleague, not a vendor.** The site speaks as Asterisk in first person ("Hi, I'm Asterisk") and as a team that felt the pain ("The compliance team we always wanted").
- **Plain and specific.** Short sentences, concrete tasks ("Chased customers for missing documents"), no buzzwords.
- **Calm about compliance.** No fear, no hype. Confident and matter-of-fact.
- **Respectful of what the buyer built.** "Your form", "your vendor", "your team", "your agent".

## Do
- Name the actual task (clear screening matches, chase documents, answer the bank's questionnaire).
- Use Nigerian terms: CBN, NFIU, STR, CAC, BVN, NIN, partner bank, MLRO.
- Sound like someone who has done compliance work.
- Plain English; spell out acronyms on first use if the reader may not be a specialist (CEO, CTO).

## Don't
- No em dashes, en dashes, exclamation marks (see knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml).
- No "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "seamless", "leverage", "unlock".
- No claims of certification, guaranteed outcomes or "replace your team".
- No US-only terms in Nigerian copy ("sponsor bank", "cash reporting line", "CTR" without context).

## Key phrases (from the site)
- "The AI compliance team for fintechs"
- "A new kind of compliance team, for you and your agents"
- "thinks ahead like a compliance officer ... handles the reviews, decisions and follow-up like an analyst"
- "Whatever you've built internally stays, with no migration."
- "Work with it where you already are."
- "You only pay for what you use."
- "Your vendor stays"
