## Banned

### Banned angles
- "Replace your compliance team" or anything implying job cuts.
- CBN certification, approval or "compliant with the baseline standards". Not established.
- Data residency claims (e.g. "your data stays in Nigeria") until confirmed.
- "Asterisk doesn't hold your data" or "we don't store data". Contradicted by Asterisk's own API docs, which describe stored and retained records.
- Fear-mongering about fines or the 2024 bans aimed at the prospect.
- Diagnosing the prospect's team as failing.

### Banned words (company-specific)
- "sponsor bank" in Nigerian copy (use "partner bank").
- "AI-powered" as a lead adjective (every competitor says it).

### Never in copy
- Existing customer names or volumes; Serai, Inc. internal structure; Karsa wind-down details.

## Voice notes
See [brand.md](../brand-kit/brand.md). From Shahryar's real prospect email (2026-11-19):
- Opens by naming himself plainly: "I'm Shahryar, founder of Asterisk."
- Describes the product in plain words, then gives one concrete example task.
- Closes with interest in them: "I'd love to show you what we've built and hear how you're running compliance."
- Cold copy keeps this voice but is shorter (under 120 words) and has no calendar link in email 1.

## CTA ladder
- **Primary (confirmed 2026-11-13):** a call with the team to learn about their setup and see whether there's a fit. Ask as a low-friction interest question (e.g. "Worth a short call to compare notes?"), not a hard meeting push.
- **Secondary:** referral to the right compliance owner.
- **Follow-up offer:** have the team set up a sandbox for their company (built per company, never a generic link).
- Never: calendar links in email 1.
