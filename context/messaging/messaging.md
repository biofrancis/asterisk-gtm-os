# Messaging: Asterisk Nigeria

Status: DRAFT v1, 2026-11-13. Claims must trace to [facts.md](facts.md). Read [brand.md](../brand-kit/brand.md) for voice.

## Core message
Asterisk is an AI compliance team for fintechs. It does the reviews, decisions and follow-up your team does by hand, inside the tools you already use.

## Pillars

### 1. It does the work
- Clears screening matches that are different people, chases missing documents, records every decision with its reasons and sources.
- Proof needed: Nigerian or anonymised metrics (none approved yet).

### 2. Keeps what you built
- Your ID vendor, rules, onboarding flow and agents stay. No migration.
- Start with one workflow, add more when you want. Pay only for what it does.

### 3. Ready for what the CBN and your bank ask next
- Answers partner bank questionnaires from evidence already on file.
- Traces business owners and screens everyone behind them.
- Helps you automate review work ahead of the CBN's automated AML deadline. (State the deadline as a public fact only, never as Asterisk being certified.)

### 4. Thinks ahead
- Flags risks you didn't ask about, like a new customer type your review flow doesn't cover yet.

## Angles by persona
| Persona | Lead with | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| CCO / MLRO | Pillars 1 and 3: the queue and the bank | "replace your team" |
| Analyst | Pillar 1: less repetitive work | Any hint of job loss |
| CEO / COO | Scale compliance without matching headcount; pay as you go | Cost-cutting framing of their team |
| CTO | Pillar 2: API, MCP, no migration | Overpromising integrations not confirmed |

## Message-to-trigger map (for the signal matrix)
| Trigger | Message |
