# Competitors and alternatives: Asterisk Nigeria

Status: v1.1, 2026-11-13. Source: [nigeria-market-raw.md](../docs/research/nigeria-market-raw.md) section 5. Vendor claims are their own and unverified.

## Framing (country lead, 2026-11-13 [S9])
Asterisk is **proactive, not reactive**. Tools like Smartcomply and Youverify flag, verify and alert, then wait for a human to act. Asterisk sits on the **decision layer** where human judgment would otherwise be needed. It learns the company's existing compliance processes and protocols, notices patterns, monitors transactions, collects results from the other tools, makes the decision, and cites why and which source each decision feeds from. That removes the repetitive manual work an analyst would do over and over.

So the **primary competitor is the manual analyst workload** (and the hiring plan behind it). Local vendors are mostly **inputs Asterisk sits on top of**, and only partial overlap where they sell case management or monitoring.

## The real alternatives a Nigerian fintech weighs
1. **Hire more analysts.** The default. Fintechs have been hiring compliance staff and poaching from banks [N section 2]. *Our angle:* scale the work without scaling the headcount at the same rate.
2. **Build in-house.** Many fintechs built onboarding, rules and agents themselves. *Our angle:* keep all of it; Asterisk takes the manual step.
3. **Buy a local AML platform** (Smartcomply Adhere, Youverify). *Our angle:* see below.
4. **Do nothing until the 2028 deadline.**
