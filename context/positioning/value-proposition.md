Status: DRAFT v1, 2026-11-13. Workflow descriptions are quoted from the site [A: asterisk-raw.md section 2]. Nigerian pains come from [N: nigeria-market-raw.md] or are marked as hypotheses.

## By workflow

| Workflow | Site description (verbatim) | Nigerian pain it answers | Who feels it |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYB review | "Checks the business at its source, traces its owners and screens everyone behind it." | Merchant and business onboarding, UBO tracing now in a CBN circular [N] | CCO, onboarding lead |

## Gains (from the site)
- Works where the team works: Slack, email, terminal; own agents via API or MCP [A: section 3].
- No migration; pick workflows one by one, change them in chat [A: section 3].
- Decisions come back "with its sources" [A: section 3].
- Pay as you go with no minimum, or plans from $500 a month [A: section 4].

## Pain relievers to prove (no numbers until approved)
- Fewer hours on false screening matches and document chasing.
- Faster business onboarding.
- Faster, evidence-backed answers to partner banks.
